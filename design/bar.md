# bar.md — what "best" means for this portfolio

References (all measured live, 1440×900):
- **A — jhey.dev** (Jhey Tompkins, ex-Google Chrome DevRel): #fff canvas, 2 colours (grey ink + red), 3 type sizes (58/16/14px), 600px text column, ~18% of the first screen carries text.
- **B — addyosmani.com** (Addy Osmani, 14 yrs Google): display name 96px = 6× the 16px body; the only colour is a highlight on key terms; bio reads like a résumé.
- **C — jasonyuan.design** (Jason Yuan, ex-Apple design studio): 2 type sizes, 2 colours, strict 3-column grid, ~13% text coverage.

Every line below is checkable by looking at a screenshot.

1. **Day and night.** (updated 2026-09-24 at the owner's request, replacing the old monochrome rule) Light mode is a sunny day: warm white canvas (#fffdf8), sky-blue gradient at the top, the sun in the top-right corner, drifting clouds, the portrait in full colour. Dark mode is night: deep navy canvas (#060918), moon top-right, twinkling stars + shooting stars, a faint aurora, the portrait in black & white. Body text stays near-black / near-white.
2. **Colour, rationed.** One accent per theme (day: burnt orange #c2410c + sky blue; night: violet #a78bfa + cyan) for numbers, labels, the primary button and hovers. The yellow marker still appears on at most 3 facts per screen.
3. **Big and bold, one family.** Headlines are one bold sans (weight ≥ 700, as heavy as reference B's 96px name). The hero name is at least 6× the body size; section titles at least 3×. One type family for headlines and text (a mono is allowed only for small labels). Any single screen uses at most 4 distinct type sizes.
4. **The 10-second recruiter test.** Without scrolling at 1440×900, a reader can find: name, role, current employer, location, availability window, and one-click résumé + email. At 390×844 the same facts are visible within 1.5 screens.
5. **Restraint.** Body text columns are ≤ 640px wide. Text covers ≤ 30% of the first screen; the rest is whitespace or the 3D object.
6. **Grid.** Every section aligns to one shared column grid (max 3 columns at desktop). Sections are separated by hairlines and whitespace — no boxed cards with shadows, no pill chips.
7. **One 3D object, on-brand.** The hero has exactly one 3D element: the L logo mark in the theme accent (orange by day, violet by night) with an orbit ring + dust, lit from the top right where the sun/moon sits. It sways slowly, follows the pointer, spins on drag, never overlaps text, and disappears cleanly under reduced motion or on low-power devices.
