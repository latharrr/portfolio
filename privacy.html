Who runs this site

This is the personal website of Deepanshu Lathar, based in Delhi NCR, India. This policy covers deepanshulathar.com except the pages under /labs, which have their own policy.

Analytics

The home page loads two analytics tools once the page has finished loading:

  • Google Analytics 4, which counts visits and records pages viewed, the site you came from, your device and browser type, and your approximate location worked out from your IP address.
  • Microsoft Clarity, which records how the page is used: clicks, scrolling and pointer movement. It can replay a visit as a recording and build heatmaps from many visits.

Both set cookies in your browser and send this information to Google and Microsoft, who process it under their own privacy policies. I use it only to see which parts of the site are read and to fix what is not working. I do not use it for advertising, and I do not sell or share it.

This policy page and the pages under /labs load no analytics.

Turning analytics off

Analytics does not load at all if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. You can also switch it off for this browser here:

The choice is remembered on this device only. Cookies already set by an earlier visit stay until they expire or you clear them in your browser settings.

The AI terminal

The home page has a terminal that answers questions about me. When you ask one, your question and up to three earlier questions and answers from the same visit are sent to my server and on to Groq, the AI model provider, to write the reply. Groq processes it under its own privacy policy.

  • Questions and answers are not stored by this site.
  • Your IP address is held in memory for a short time to limit each visitor to 15 questions a day. It is not written to a database.
  • Please do not type personal or sensitive information into the terminal.

GitHub data

The open-source section shows my public GitHub activity. It is normally fetched by my server. If that fails, your browser asks GitHub directly, and my profile picture is always loaded from GitHub, so GitHub may see your IP address.

Stored on your device

  • Your light or dark theme choice, so the site opens the way you left it.
  • Your analytics choice, if you use the switch above.
  • A short-lived copy of GitHub data for the current tab, only when your browser had to fetch it directly.

These stay in your browser and are not sent to me.

Hosting and links

The site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and the pages requested, to keep the site running.

Links to email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, GitHub and my projects open those services, under their own policies. If you email or message me, I receive what you send and use it only to reply.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal data I hold about you, or withdraw consent, at any time, including under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Email deepanshu.lathar [at] gmail.com and I will respond within 30 days.

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date at the top of it.
