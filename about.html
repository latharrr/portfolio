What I do now

I start with the problem: what a founder or user needs to decide. Then the decisions: what to build first, and what to measure.

Then I build the first version. In PicaPool's Founder's Office that means AI systems, internal tools, analytics and attribution. At UniLyf it meant using behaviour and retention data to shape product and launch decisions. I also build my own products, ProofMart, Gapl and LpuTV, and run Lathar Labs, a small software studio.
