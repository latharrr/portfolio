How the work gets done

Proof of efficiency

At PicaPool I shipped 15+ production internal tools in 8 weeks: a CRM with role-based access, a WhatsApp Business API dashboard, campaign attribution and merchant onboarding, with the CTO advising on database design.

The attribution and CRM tooling then carried a two-month campus push that reached 3,000+ students. A separate campus launch collected 1,000+ submissions in 72 hours.

Delivery and deadlines

I ship in small, working slices. Each one is deployed, so you see a running URL, not a status update. Daily KPI digests and n8n workflows keep the numbers in front of the team without anyone opening a dashboard.

Three things I hold together

Build: TypeScript, Next.js, Node, Postgres, Supabase, Docker.

Measure: Event fingerprinting, cohort retention, funnels, A/B tests.

Ship: Vercel deploys, real users, launch plans and a team to run them.

Use cases

AI on your data: a read-only analyst over a 79-table Postgres schema. Validated, read-only queries with guardrails on what can run.

Document checks: ProofMart runs Tesseract OCR and checks running balances, carry-forwards and duplicates.

Demand validation: at UniLyf, cohort analysis across 15 deeply profiled beta users isolated the actions tied to Day-7 retention.
