Gapl

Candidates get rejected without ever learning why. Gapl simulates a recruiter's screen of your résumé against a target role and company tier: ATS fit, readiness, the exact gaps. Then it builds a week-by-week roadmap to close them.

If one provider fails, the scan falls back

Every analysis goes through a multi-model AI gateway that fails over across Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini.

Email links are rewritten to a tracking subdomain by Next.js middleware for 30-day last-touch attribution.

Test result: evaluated the résumé pipeline on a 44-document synthetic test set. Found and fixed a false-rejection bug: 41/44 to 44/44. Pipeline test result, not real-user accuracy.

What a scan returns

Four answers, in the order a recruiter would reach them.

01

ATS fit

How the résumé reads to the screening software for that role.

02

Readiness

Where you stand against the target role and company tier.

03

The exact gaps

What a recruiter would see missing, named plainly.

04

A roadmap

A week-by-week plan to close those gaps.
