# Design System: Deepanshu Labs

Since 23 October 2026 Deepanshu Labs uses the portfolio's design system. See `/DESIGN.md` at the repo root, especially the "Labs components" section: the hanging sign, the example chat, steps, the live chip and the phone dock. The earlier "Open Sign" identity (dark bar, red and amber fields, Archivo) is retired, and its `labs.css`, `labs.js` and font were removed.

Labs-specific rules that still hold:

- The fastest route to WhatsApp wins every layout decision. "Try it on WhatsApp" appears in the hero, after the steps, in the last promise card, in the contact strip and, on phones, in the dock.
- No analytics, cookies or forms on Labs pages (see `labs/PRODUCT.md`).
- The example chat is labelled as an example.
- Pages are generated by `tools/build-labs.py`; edit copy there, then run `python3 tools/sync-pages.py` and `python3 tools/inline-css.py`.
