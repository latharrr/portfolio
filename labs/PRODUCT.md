## Users

**Primary: owners of small businesses that answer customers on WhatsApp.** Clinics, salons, shops, trades and similar. They are usually on a phone, between customers, with messages going unanswered while they work. Their job on this page is to find out, quickly and without a sales call, whether an AI can answer their customers properly.

They are not technical and do not judge the product by the builder's engineering background.

## Product Purpose

Deepanshu Labs is an independent software studio run by Deepanshu Lathar. It is the umbrella, not a single product: projects ship under it, and more will be added. `/labs` is the studio's home and lists every live project.

The first project is the **WhatsApp AI Receptionist** (`/labs/whatsapp-receptionist`): it replies to a business's customers in seconds, answers their questions, and books the next step into the diary. It is set up around each business's own services, prices and hours.

The receptionist page exists to get a business owner to try the live demo. Success is a visitor who taps through to WhatsApp, messages the demo line, and sees it book a real call.

## Positioning

The demo is the product, running live. A visitor messages a real WhatsApp number and a real Google Meet link lands in the chat within minutes (delivery can take up to about 15 minutes, so the page says "within minutes"), which is the same flow their own customers would get. A competitor with a video or a mock-up could not copy this claim.

Second: one person, not an agency. The person the visitor messages is the person who builds and fixes it.

## Operating Context

- The visitor arrives on a phone far more often than a laptop, and the main action opens WhatsApp.
- Primary action: "Try it on WhatsApp", which opens a chat with the demo line, +91 99996 02901, with a prefilled message asking to book a call.
- Secondary: message Deepanshu directly on WhatsApp, or email.
- The demo line is live 24/7 (the owner confirmed on 2026-10-13 that he tested the full booking flow outside the old 08:00 to 23:30 hours; the page no longer shows hours or an open/closed state).
- "Message me on WhatsApp" opens Deepanshu's own number, +91 82210 16809, which is separate from the AI demo line.
- The demo books a call with Deepanshu: the visitor gives a day, a time and a topic; the assistant asks for anything missing, one short question at a time; a Google Meet link with the date, time and both names arrives in the chat.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Confirmed capabilities (from the current page):**

- Replies on WhatsApp in seconds and answers customer questions.
- Books the next step into the diary; in the demo, a call with a real Google Meet link.
- Set up per business: services, prices, hours.
- Does not pretend to be human: when asked, it says it is an AI assistant.
- The owner is told right away when someone books.

**Constraints:**

- The page sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. The privacy policy says so; the page must stay that way unless the policy changes first. (The only browser storage is the shared animation-pause choice, held for the tab.)
- The privacy policy at `/labs/privacy` is legal text. It is carried word for word; it was last updated, and its effective date set, on 23 Oct 2026 (fonts and images served from this site).
- The site sits under `/labs` on deepanshulathar.com and shares the L mark with the portfolio.
- Structure: `/labs` (studio home, project list), `/labs/<project>` (one page per project), `/labs/privacy`, `/labs/404`. A new project gets its own page and a row on the studio home.
- The owner asked for the hero to show only "Live 24/7", without the booking and setup rows, and for the example chat to be colourful in the way WhatsApp looks.
- The builder's engineering background (repo counts, project gallery, tech stacks, portrait) is deliberately not on this page. It lives on the portfolio.

**Undecided:**

- Pricing, setup time and supported languages are not stated anywhere and must not be invented.
- Whether the page should name a country. The earlier copy addressed UK trades and priced in pounds; the audience is now any small business.
- What the studio says about itself beyond one sentence; future projects are not named anywhere.

## Product Principles

1. **The demo is the pitch.** The fastest route to WhatsApp wins every layout decision.
2. **Phone first.** The page is read one-handed, in a gap between customers.
3. **Say only what the live assistant does.** Claims match the demo's real behaviour.
4. **Plain words.** No jargon, no model names, nothing a shop owner would have to look up.
5. **Honest labels.** An example is called an example; an AI is called an AI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No standard has been set by the owner. The current page has a skip link and labelled controls; the rebuild keeps them, honours reduced motion, and holds text contrast at 4.5:1 or better.
