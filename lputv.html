LpuTV

60,000 students on one campus, and meeting anyone outside your class still depends on luck. LpuTV is a photo booth for conversation: talk to a random student for ten minutes, and nobody sees who you are until you both say yes.

How it runs

Socket.IO handles matching and chat, PeerJS/WebRTC carries peer-to-peer video, and every message is rate-limited and sanitised.

Where it runs

On the web, and as an Android app via Capacitor. Sign in with a college Google account for a verified badge. It started life as UniConnect.
