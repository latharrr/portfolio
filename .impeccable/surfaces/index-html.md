## Direction contract

THESIS: His work drawn as a transit map. Engineering, growth and writing are lines that cross at an interchange; projects and roles are stations whose drawing states their true status. Refuses the split hero of copy beside a portrait with a row of project cards beneath.

OWN-WORLD: Map paper #fbfaf5, ink #1a1a1a, and three route colours: blue #0a5fb4 (engineering), yellow #f5c400 (growth), magenta #c2187a (writing). Thick octilinear route lines, ink-ringed station roundels, a double-ring interchange, dashed track for unreleased work, a barred roundel for an offline demo, lettered exit signs for the two actions, line lozenges as tags. Whole sections are drenched in one route colour. One face: Overpass, the open Highway Gothic; Overpass Mono for code and data only.

STORY: The visitor sees who he is and where he is now, understands that he works across three disciplines, checks a project's status at a glance, and leaves through the résumé or email exit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name at top left in two lines at roughly a tenth of the viewport width, role and lede under it. Blue and yellow enter from the left edge and rise together into a double-ring interchange right of the surname, labelled PicaPool, now. Blue runs east through ProofMart and Gapl, then climbs to College-CLI on dashed track. Yellow drops south-east to LPU Admissions. Magenta rises from the bottom edge through the interchange and exits north-east past 30 days of writing. Exit A, download résumé, and Exit B, email, sit bottom left. Now, based in and available sit bottom right as plain text.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: On load the three lines draw outward from the interchange and stations seat in order, once. Every station is a link that lights in its line colour and travels to its section. The header marks the current stop. Nothing else animates on entry.

FORM: Line Map, candidate 1 of the second hand's ordered list, chosen by the owner as the pick. Seed key 871703fc.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
