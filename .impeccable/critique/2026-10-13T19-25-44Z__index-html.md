## Design specificity

Hero is authored (a transit map of real projects with true status). Below it: reusable slogans as headings, LinkedIn-style About copy. The experiment's pale tints and scroll blend made it less specific.
Detector: 18 advisory findings (12 font sizes off the documented ramp, 3 undocumented colours, 1 radius, dashed-track gradient, 48 em-dashes). Browser scan added line-length (~94 chars) on the Projects intro.

## Priority issues

1. [P1] Scroll colour blend hurts legibility and looks broken. Text polarity flips seven times; 55-75 elements dip under contrast mid-blend in light; Writing fails at rest (3.64:1 light, 3.39:1 dark) for .sub, .post p, .post .kind; hero buttons fade to ~0.55 while visible. Fix: remove the blend and the .fly fade; restore solid section grounds; restore Writing secondary text colour. Command: quieter.
2. [P1] Portrait and contact terminus never render in Chrome: [data-enter] elements are fully clipped by their own clip-path so the IntersectionObserver never fires. Fix: observe an unclipped wrapper or clip the child; add a timeout fail-safe. Command: polish.
3. [P1] Page disagrees with the résumé: PicaPool title (three variants), six-person team attribution (PicaPool vs UniLyf), UniConnect status (offline vs Live), Gapl providers, unsupported skills (C, FastAPI, MongoDB, AWS, Docker, LangChain, OpenCV), unlinked 1,000+ and 200+ claims, no graduation year or batch on the page, essays and credentials link to a generic LinkedIn feed. Command: clarify.
4. [P2] Motion clutter and bugs: trains render as white gaps (stroke resolves to line colour), parked trains ghost at the stage corner on hover, pointer tilt moves name and buttons, rail car covers roundels, line tags stay dim ~1s after hover, terminal demo loops scripted answers under an "AI" badge inside aria-live. Keep the line draw, line highlight and theme wipe. Command: quieter.
5. [P2] Too long and undersells: Experience bullets are adjectives while the résumé has numbers (79-table schema, 15+ internal tools in 8 weeks, 3,000+ students); three non-engineering campus roles; 21 phone screens with résumé only at top and bottom; 14 receipts. Command: distill.
