  1. ProofMart

    Live · beta

    Edited bank statements and invoices pass a visual check. ProofMart is a document-forensics API: upload a PDF and get back a structured report of findings — each pinned to its place on the page, each with the arithmetic that proves it.

    Technical decision: check the document’s own math instead of guessing at pixels — running balances, cross-page carry-forwards, duplicate transactions — so every flag is explainable. Tesseract OCR covers scanned pages; Next.js route handlers expose it all as an API.

    About 9,200 lines of TypeScript, covered by 15 Vitest suites and 4 Playwright end-to-end specs. Reports are signed with Ed25519; webhooks are HMAC-verified.

    • Next.js
    • TypeScript
    • Supabase
    • pdf.js
    • Tesseract.js
    • Vercel Blob
    • Razorpay

    Source Live — proofmart.shop

  2. Gapl

    Live · personal project

    Candidates get rejected without ever learning why. Gapl simulates a recruiter’s screen of your resume against a target role and company tier — ATS fit, readiness, the exact gaps — then builds a week-by-week roadmap to close them.

    Technical decision: every analysis goes through a multi-model AI gateway that fails over across Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, so one provider’s outage never fails a scan. Email links are rewritten to a tracking subdomain by Next.js middleware for 30-day last-touch attribution.

    • Next.js
    • TypeScript
    • Firebase
    • Firestore
    • Groq
    • OpenAI
    • Anthropic
    • Gemini
    • Razorpay
    • Resend

    Source Live — gapl.vercel.app

  3. College-CLI

    Working · pre-release

    Students lose the first week of every course to installing the wrong tools. College-CLI maps a course to exactly what it needs — npx college-cli lpu/cse-301 detects, plans, installs and verifies the setup on a fresh laptop.

    Technical decision: courses are data, not code — a schema-validated registry fetched from GitHub, cached for 24 hours, with a bundled fallback for campus proxies. A version newer than the course allows is reported as a conflict, not a doomed install.

    • TypeScript
    • Node.js
    • YAML registry
    • Windows preflight
    • GitHub Actions

    Source No live link yet — npm release pending

  4. UniConnect LPU Connect

    Built · demo offline

    60,000 students on one campus, and meeting anyone outside your class still depends on luck. UniConnect is anonymous campus chat and video: random ten-minute matches, with identities revealed only when both people opt in.

    Technical decision: privacy by mechanism — a mutual “Enjoy” unlocks friend requests before any name is shown. Socket.IO handles matching and chat, PeerJS/WebRTC carries peer-to-peer video, and every message is rate-limited and sanitised. Ships as an Android app via Capacitor.

    • React 19
    • Vite
    • Socket.IO
    • WebRTC
    • Express 5
    • SQLite
    • Capacitor

    Source No live demo — currently offline

From G15 to MacBook Air

Five years ago, my family bought me an Asus ROG G15 — on sale, and still more than they should have spent. They never once told me to pick something cheaper. They asked what I actually needed, and paid for it.

I told them then: the next one is on me. In January I gave myself six months to make that true. The deadline was July.

On 15 August — six weeks late, but here, and mine — the G15 retired. Its replacement: a MacBook Air M5, bought with my own money.

It was never once the reason something didn’t ship. Neither is the new one.
